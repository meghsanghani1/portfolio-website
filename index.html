Frequently Asked Questions

A closer look at my experience, approach to performance marketing, and how I think about growth.

I’m Megh Sanghani, a digital and performance marketing professional with 5+ years of experience across strategy, execution, optimization and digital growth.

My academic background combines technical and business thinking. I hold an M.Tech in Chemical Engineering from PDEU, Gandhinagar, and an MBA in Sales and Marketing from Amity University.

Over the years, I’ve worked across 18+ industries, with both domestic and international clients, and contributed to 200+ projects. My core experience spans Meta Ads, Google Ads, Google Analytics, SEO including on-page, off-page, technical and local SEO, email marketing, and WhatsApp marketing.

What interests me most is bringing performance marketing, data, technology, creative thinking and AI together to build marketing systems that are more measurable, efficient and scalable.

I’m primarily looking for Senior or Manager-level opportunities in performance marketing, paid media, growth marketing or digital marketing.

I’m interested in roles where I can take greater ownership of strategy, campaign execution, experimentation, optimization and performance measurement, while contributing to broader business growth.

I’m particularly drawn to environments where marketing decisions are connected to data, customer behaviour and measurable business outcomes.

I bring a combination of analytical thinking, hands-on execution and cross-channel marketing experience.

I’m comfortable moving between strategy, campaign setup, audience and creative testing, optimization, analytics and reporting while keeping the larger business objective in view.

Having worked across different industries, audiences and business models, I’ve also learned to adapt the approach to the market rather than applying the same marketing playbook everywhere.

I start by understanding the business objective, target audience, offer, customer journey and success metrics.

From there, I develop the campaign structure, define the targeting and creative approach, identify what needs to be tested, and ensure the required tracking and measurement are in place before launch.

Once the campaign is live, I monitor performance across the funnel, identify opportunities or constraints, and optimize based on both campaign data and business impact.

Before launching, I look at the business objective, audience, offer, competitive context, creative, landing-page experience, tracking setup, budget and measurement framework.

I also evaluate how the campaign fits into the complete customer journey.

A strong advertising campaign alone cannot compensate for issues with the offer, landing page, tracking, sales process or conversion experience, so I prefer looking at the system as a whole before spending begins.

I prefer hypothesis-led testing rather than changing multiple variables without a clear reason.

Before a test, I define what is being tested, why I believe the change could improve performance, which metric will determine the outcome and what level of data is needed before making a decision.

Depending on the campaign, testing could involve creative, messaging, audiences, offers, landing pages, campaign structure or bidding approaches.

The objective is not simply to run more tests, but to generate insights that improve future decisions.

I look at performance consistency, conversion quality, efficiency, available data and underlying business economics.

If a campaign is consistently meeting the required targets and has room to grow, I evaluate opportunities to scale it gradually.

If performance is below expectations, I first diagnose the constraint and test improvements rather than reacting immediately.

And when the evidence consistently shows that an approach is not viable, I’m comfortable stopping it and reallocating the budget toward stronger opportunities.

I use AI across different parts of the marketing workflow, including research, ideation, analysis, creative development, data interpretation, automation and experimentation.

It helps me explore ideas faster, reduce repetitive work, analyse information more efficiently and evaluate more possibilities.

I see AI as an accelerator rather than a substitute for marketing judgment. Final decisions still need to be grounded in performance data, customer context, experience and business objectives.

I avoid judging a campaign based on one metric or a short-term fluctuation.

Instead, I break performance down across the funnel and look at targeting, creative, messaging, offer, landing page, tracking, conversion rate, lead quality and business economics.

Sometimes the advertising itself is performing correctly and the actual constraint exists elsewhere, such as pricing, the product, the landing-page experience, sales follow-up or the wider customer journey.

The objective is to identify the real bottleneck before deciding what needs to change.

I don’t look at data simply to create reports.

I look for patterns, changes, anomalies and opportunities across the funnel and then ask one important question: What should we do differently because of what the data is telling us?

That might mean reallocating budget, testing a new creative direction, changing targeting, improving the landing page, adjusting an offer or investigating another part of the customer journey.

For me, reporting becomes valuable when it leads to a clear decision or next action.

I believe performance marketing is most effective when media, creative, content, web and sales teams are aligned around the same business objective.

I use campaign data, audience behaviour and performance trends to provide teams with clear insights they can act on.

With creative teams, that may involve identifying stronger hooks, formats or messages. With sales teams, it can mean understanding lead quality, objections or conversion gaps. With content and web teams, it may involve improving landing pages, messaging or the broader customer journey.

I see performance marketing as a cross-functional growth discipline, not an isolated advertising function.

Performance marketing, advertising platforms and AI evolve quickly, so I stay current through a combination of industry updates, platform developments and hands-on experimentation.

I prefer testing new tools, features and workflows myself rather than adopting something simply because it is trending.

Currently, I’m particularly interested in practical applications of AI, automation, data and creative experimentation that can improve marketing efficiency, decision-making and campaign execution.

The focus is always on finding applications that create genuine value rather than using technology for its own sake.
