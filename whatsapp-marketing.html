Generating Real Estate Enquiries Through WhatsApp

A WhatsApp broadcast programme designed to generate property enquiries and site-visit intent across multiple real estate campaigns. The mix included project-launch communication, 2 BHK promotions, premium inventory pushes, weekend site-visit campaigns, limited-time offers, retargeting for interested prospects and commercial-spaces outreach. Performance was evaluated through recipients, sent volume, delivery and read behaviour.
