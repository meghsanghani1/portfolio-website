I try not to make decisions based on one metric or a short term fluctuation. I break the campaign down across the funnel and look at areas such as targeting, creative, offer, landing page, tracking, conversion rate, and overall economics. Once I understand where the problem is, I prioritize the changes that are most likely to have an impact and test them systematically.

I think this is one of the most important parts of performance marketing. Sometimes the ads are actually doing their job, but something further down the customer journey is affecting the result.

For example, if the campaign is bringing the right audience at a reasonable cost but people are not converting, I would look beyond the ad account. The issue could be the offer, pricing, landing page, product, sales process, or customer experience. I try to look at the complete picture instead of assuming every problem is an advertising problem.

I don't look at data only to create reports. I look for patterns, changes, anomalies, and opportunities across the funnel. From there, I try to answer a simple question: what should we do differently because of what the data is telling us? That could mean testing a new creative, changing the audience, reallocating budget, improving the landing page, or investigating a specific part of the funnel.
